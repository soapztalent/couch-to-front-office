# Real NHL market comparables (researched Oct 8, 2026)

Everything below is a completed, sourced trade. Nothing is estimated. Pick numbers are the real draft slots where they're known.

## Fabian Lysell's most recent trade
- **June 27, 2026: Boston traded Fabian Lysell to Colorado for forward Ivan Ivan**, a straight one-for-one. Ivan is 23, an undrafted depth/AHL centre (5 points in 49 career NHL games). Lysell was 23 and a pending RFA who spent all of 2025-26 in the AHL (42 points in 57 games), with 12 career NHL games. Sportsnet called it "swapping AHL forwards." Colorado then signed him to a one-year deal on July 13, 2026.
  Sources: [NHL.com, Avalanche Acquire Lysell](https://www.nhl.com/avalanche/news/avalanche-acquire-lysell), [Sportsnet](https://www.sportsnet.ca/nhl/article/bruins-acquire-ivan-ivan-from-avalanche-for-fabian-lysell/), [Boston.com](https://www.boston.com/sports/boston-bruins/2026/06/27/boston-bruins-nhl-hockey-fabian-lysell-trade-ivan-ivan-colorado/), [NHL.com, Avalanche Signs Lysell](https://www.nhl.com/avalanche/news/avalanche-signs-lysell)
- **What it means for the game:** the real price for Lysell was one depth forward and no picks at all. The in-game deal (STL sent two 1sts plus Sundqvist to COL for him) was off by at least an order of magnitude.

**Pattern:** a single 1st, usually a late one and often conditional or protected, buys one top-6 forward or top-4 D rental. Two 1sts only change hands for a star (Rantanen, Meier) or for two real NHL pieces in one deal (Gourde + Bjorkstrand). Lesser rentals like Tanev or Coyle go for 2nds and 3rds. No contender gave a 1st for a depth player.

## Young RFAs and pick-for-player trades in the offseason
- **Alex Newhook**, 21, middle-six RFA (COL→MTL, June 27, 2023): #31 (a 1st) + #37 (2nd) + Gianni Fairbrother ([NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/montreal-acquires-alex-newhook-from-colorado-in-exchange-for-draft-pic-345043034)). A young player with real NHL production cost a late 1st plus an early 2nd.
- **JJ Peterka** (UTA→BOS, June 26, 2026): #23 + a 2028 1st. **Mason McTavish** (ANA→STL, June 26, 2026): #15 + #29. **Pavel Dorofeyev** (VGK→NYR, June 26, 2026): #26 + 2028 1st + #92 ([NHL.com 2025-26 tracker](https://www.nhl.com/news/topic/trade-coverage/2025-26-nhl-trades)). Two 1sts is the price for an established young top-6 scorer, not for an AHL depth RFA.
- **Pick-for-pick swaps** at the 2026 draft: #31 for two 2nds (#42 and #57); #21 for #27 + #62 + #120 (same tracker). A late 1st is worth about two 2nds.
- **Official CBA scale: 2026 offer-sheet compensation** (AAV → picks): under $1.58M nothing; up to $2.39M a 3rd; up to $4.78M a 2nd; up to $7.16M a 1st + 3rd; up to $9.55M a 1st + 2nd + 3rd; up to $11.94M two 1sts + 2nd + 3rd; above that four 1sts ([PHR](https://www.prohockeyrumors.com/2026/05/nhl-sets-offer-sheet-thresholds-for-2026.html), [Sportsnet](https://www.sportsnet.ca/nhl/article/compensation-thresholds-for-2026-nhl-offer-sheets-revealed/)). Under the league's own scale, two 1sts is what you pay for a player earning about $9.5M or more.

## How the game's new scale lines up
- **A 1st ≈ a top-6 F / top-4 D in his prime:** late 1st 246, mid 286, top-10 337+, #1 540. For comparison, Geekie (83, 28) is 243, Lindholm (84) 262 and McAvoy (89) 382.
- **Two 1sts ≈ a star:** two mid 1sts come to about 570, close to Pastrnak at about 500.
- **A 2nd ≈ a solid prospect:** 97 to 152.
- **A depth player under 81 OVR without a believable top-6 ceiling** is capped at 0.75 × a mid 1st (about 215), so no 1st can be given for him.
