The shipped mesh keeps 1,649,999 triangles from the original 4,999,906-triangle
photogrammetry scan. It is not a subdivided mannequin. The albedo is the scan
photograph. The normal map is the high-frequency surface of that photograph
(skin grain, jacket quilting, denim). Roughness separates skin, the jacket,
and the jeans so the light responds differently on each.

Changes: decimated for the real-time file size, oriented to face +Z with feet
on the floor, and given a normal map and a roughness map. No endorsement by
the subject or Digital Reality Lab is implied.
