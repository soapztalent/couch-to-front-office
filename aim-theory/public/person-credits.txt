person.glb is an original character built from MakeHuman Community assets.

The body, skin, short hair, eyes, eyelashes, eyebrows, button shirt, jeans,
belt, and shoes are MakeHuman system assets released as CC0 in September 2020
by Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, and Jonas Hauquier. The character is not
a copied game character and not a photogrammetry scan.

The mesh is posed as a standing guard: both feet planted, weight even, fists
up and empty. No phone and no run cycle. After the color and normal maps were
in place, the body, shirt, and shoes were Catmull-Clark subdivided with the
UVs kept. The shipped file has 1,335,648 triangles.

Skin, shirt, jeans, and shoes use real color maps. Skin pores were turned into
a tangent-space normal from that color map. The shirt, jeans, and shoes use
their authored tangent-space normal maps. Lighting samples those maps with the
mesh tangents.

The figure faces +Z. The feet sit on Y = 0. Height is 1.80 m.
