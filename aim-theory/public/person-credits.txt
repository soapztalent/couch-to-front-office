The shipped mesh keeps 1,650,000 triangles from the original 4,999,935-triangle
photogrammetry scan. It is not a subdivided mannequin and it is not a copied
game character. The albedo is the scan photograph. Lighting uses the scan's
geometric normals. An image-space normal map is not shipped, because that map
painted black streaks along hair, brows, and clothing seams.

The pose is the scan as captured: a forward run with both fists up and empty,
eyes ahead, no phone and no prop. The rear foot is off the floor because that
is the running stride. The mesh was decimated for the real-time file, oriented
to face +Z with the planted foot on the floor, and scaled to 1.80 m so it
matches the range camera (the scanned subject is 163 cm).
