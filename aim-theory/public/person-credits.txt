The body, skin, short hair, eyes, eyelashes, button shirt, jeans,
belt, and shoes are MakeHuman system assets released as CC0 in September 2020
by Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, and Jonas Hauquier. The character is not
a copied game character and not a photogrammetry scan.

The mesh is posed as a standing guard: both feet planted, weight even, fists
up and empty. No phone and no run cycle. Each finger is thinned along its own
axis so the digits stay apart. After the color and normal maps were in place,
the head, shirt, and shoes were Catmull-Clark subdivided with the UVs kept.
The hands stay at the base mesh so the digits do not weld together. The
shipped file has 1,278,036 triangles.

Skin, shirt, jeans, and shoes use real color maps. Skin pores were turned into
a tangent-space normal from that color map, strong enough to read on the face.
The hands use that skin color without the pore normal, so the gaps between
the fingers stay visible. The shirt, jeans, and shoes use their authored
tangent-space normal maps. The brows are painted on the skin. The irises are
brown. Lighting samples those maps with the mesh tangents.

The figure faces +Z. The feet sit on Y = 0. Height is 1.80 m.
