A yellow cab, a slippery tablet, and the streets of fictional Nukkadpur.

Passenger address:

Driver sees the road and never the route. Arrows steer, drive, brake. Space is the horn.
Passenger holds the tablet. The yellow line is the true gali. Yell left, right, or straight before the fork.
Cabin throws mud on the glass, opens the door, and will yeet the tablet if you don’t grip it.
Street has cows who ignore horns, a wedding band that does not, chai, ladoos, and a dhol.
